The framework

Three connected dimensions.

Income, Credit and Equity are not separate topics. They are three views of the same decision.

I

Income

Understand your cash flow and financial flexibility — the money available to support daily needs, obligations, savings and future goals.

C

Credit

Understand borrowing and the consequences of debt — borrowing capacity, obligations, and how using credit shapes future options.

E

Equity

Understand ownership and long-term value — equity built in a home or other assets, and the decisions that create, use or reduce it.

Why I.C.E. is different

Understanding over shortcuts.

I.C.E. is not a list of rules to memorize. It is a structured way of thinking — so you can weigh trade-offs, recognize long-term consequences and make more intentional decisions.

  • Look past the headline number to the whole decision.
  • See how one choice moves cash flow, obligations and ownership at once.
  • Learn through practical examples and real-world household scenarios.
  • Accept that there is no financially perfect decision — only trade-offs, understood clearly.

Never evaluate a three-dimensional decision with one-dimensional thinking.

Inside this lesson
  • Good advice can conflict: pay cash vs. never touch savings; buy vs. rent; invest vs. pay off debt.
